About the App

BMI Calculator is a simple and easy-to-use Body Mass Index (BMI) tool that helps you quickly assess your weight health and provides personalized health recommendations based on your age and gender.

Key Features

  • Enter weight, height, and age to calculate your BMI instantly
  • Male/female gender selection for more accurate results
  • Visual display of your BMI health range
  • Health improvement tips for different BMI categories

FAQ

Q: How do I calculate my BMI?
Enter your weight (kg), height (cm), and age, select your gender, then tap "Complete" to get your result.

Q: How do I interpret my BMI result?
The app uses WHO standards to categorize your result into underweight, normal, overweight, or obese ranges, with corresponding recommendations.

Q: Is my data uploaded?
All calculations are performed locally on your device. We do not collect or upload your personal health data.
